Core Features

Every restart tells a completely different story

🎴

Talent Draw System

Randomly draw talent combinations each life. From "Born Rich" to "Amazing Physique", different talents create completely different life paths.

🎲

Random Event System

300+ carefully designed life events covering childhood, school, career, and old age. Every choice affects your attribute development.

🏆

Achievement Collection

47 achievements waiting to be unlocked. From "Short-Lived" to "Chosen One", record every highlight and low point of your lives.

📊

Attribute Growth System

Five attributes — Charisma, Intelligence, Strength, Wealth, Happiness — influence each other and grow dynamically.

🌐

Multi-language Support

Switch between English and Chinese, allowing more players around the world to enjoy the fun of life restart.

Local Save System

Game progress auto-saves to local folder. Supports save management, import/export, continue your journey anytime.

How to Play

Simple and intuitive gameplay with endless possibilities

Draw Talents

Randomly draw talents each life, from common to legendary. Choose your initial advantages to shape your foundation.

Freely allocate initial attribute points. Charisma, Intelligence, Strength, Wealth, Happiness — determine your starting point.

Random events trigger every year, from childhood to old age. Every choice you make affects the direction of your destiny.

Meet specific conditions to unlock achievements. Record every highlight moment and carry them to your next life.

Keep Restarting

Accumulate talents and legacy, challenge higher scores, unlock more hidden content, experience the full spectrum of life.
